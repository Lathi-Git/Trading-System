System Rules & Ratios

The fixed logic behind every checklist item. Nothing here is discretionary…

Instruments & priority

  • DXY first: analysed for dollar bias.
  • 1st priority: XAUUSD, GBPUSD, EURUSD — analysed if news events support the currencies.
  • 2nd priority: USDCHF, USDCAD — only when news for those currencies is present that day, 9:00–17:00 SAST.
  • Crypto (weekend): BTCUSD — only traded if balance exceeds R100.

Risk & scaling

  • Base margin: 0.01 lots for every R50 of balance. Under R50 = 0.01; R50–R100 = 0.02; continues in R50 intervals.
  • Stop loss cap: max 5 pips on standard FX, 50 pips on BTCUSD, at the adjacent Fib level.
  • Take profit: min 15 pips (150 on BTCUSD), between the -61.8% and -100% expansion.
  • Breakeven: a break of 0.0% Fib moves SL to entry immediately.

15-minute HTF alignment

  • Mark 00:00 Monday external high/low. Read structure on the line chart.
  • Plot 15m PSY levels: 0.1000, 0.0750, 0.0500, 0.0250, 0.0000.
  • Trend is valid only when the 50 SMA passes through the price body leading to the BOS, not just a wick.
  • Align FVG/OB with the SMA passthrough and PSY levels.

1-minute LTF execution

  • Entry gaps run hourly 07:00–18:00 SAST. First 15 minutes: LTF confirmation.
  • Golden period: 15–30 minutes past the hour.
  • Entry at 61.8% Fib overlapping the volume profile POC, a valid POI and a 1m PSY level (0.0875, 0.0625, 0.0375, 0.0125).
